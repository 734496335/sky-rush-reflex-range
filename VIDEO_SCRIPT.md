# Demo Video Script — Sky Rush: 60-Second Reflex Range

**Target runtime:** ~2:30 (hard cap 3:00)
**Format:** screen capture of the game (desktop + one phone shot), voiceover
recorded over it. All footage from the actual game; no mockups.
**Tone:** energetic, plain-spoken. One idea per beat.

| Time | Visual (shot list) | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | **Hook.** Attract screen on a monitor, leaderboard visible. Camera pushes in toward the pulsing TAP TO START button. | "Every event booth has the same problem: a line of people, and nothing for them to do in it. This is Sky Rush — a sixty-second reflex game built to turn that line into a crowd." |
| 0:12–0:30 | **The problem.** Quick cuts: an empty booth table, a staff member shrugging at a laptop, a "network down" moment (crossed-out Wi-Fi icon drawn on a sticky note). Then the Sky Rush folder on a USB stick being plugged in. | "Booth software has to survive real booths. Unpredictable hardware. Wi-Fi that gives up. Staff who can't babysit a laptop. So Sky Rush is one static folder — no install, no server, no accounts. If a browser opens it, it runs." |
| 0:30–1:05 | **Gameplay, first 20 seconds of a round.** Screen capture: blue targets popping, hits landing, combo climbing to x2, floating score text. | "One round is sixty seconds. Tap the blue targets before they vanish. The faster the hit, the more it's worth — and every five-hit streak raises your combo multiplier, up to five times. Simple enough to learn from the line, before you ever touch the screen." |
| 1:05–1:30 | **Gameplay, mid-round.** A golden target appears and gets hit (+300 popup, chime). Then a red X decoy — the player almost taps it, pulls away. A second clip shows a decoy hit: −150, screen shake, clock drops a second. | "Golden targets pay out big. But watch for the red X — that's a decoy. Hit one and you lose a hundred fifty points, a second off the clock, and your combo. That one trap turns fast clicking into an actual skill." |
| 1:30–1:50 | **Gameplay, final stretch.** Targets spawning in groups, lifetime rings draining fast, timer hits the red zone, TIME UP. End screen: score, hits, accuracy, best combo. | "The last fifteen seconds are chaos by design — more targets, shorter fuses. When time's up, you get the full report: score, hits, accuracy, and your best combo." |
| 1:50–2:08 | **Leaderboard moment.** Initials being typed in, arcade style. The new score lands on the board; cut to the attract screen showing the updated Top Pilots list. | "Beat the board and you sign it — three initials, arcade rules. And that leaderboard lives on the attract screen, which is the whole point: the next person in line is already competing before they've played." |
| 2:08–2:22 | **Booth-proof details.** Three fast shots: (1) tab switched away mid-round → PAUSED screen; (2) game running on a phone via touch; (3) mute button tapped, speaker icon changes. | "It pauses itself if the tab loses focus. It runs the same on a touchscreen or a phone. Sound is synthesized live — there are no audio files to lose. Event staff never have to touch it." |
| 2:22–2:30 | **Closing.** Wide shot of the attract screen, TAP TO START pulsing. Title card: SKY RUSH — 60-Second Reflex Range. | "Sky Rush. Sixty seconds, zero setup, any screen. Thanks for watching — see you in the queue." |

## Production notes

- Record gameplay at 1080p with the browser window sized so the HUD is
  legible; play one genuinely good round rather than editing a fake one.
- Keep the game's own sound effects audible under the voiceover (duck
  narration ~3 dB over game audio); the golden chime and decoy buzz are
  part of the demo.
- The phone shot (2:08) can be the same `index.html` served over a local
  network or opened from file — no separate build exists, which is itself
  a selling point worth one line if runtime allows.
- End card: game title, category (Event Mini Game), and the repository
  link. No third-party logos anywhere in frame.
